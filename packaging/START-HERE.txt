TIME SERIES FORECASTING BENCH
=============================

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------

Double-click  ForecastingBench  (in this folder).

That's it. Nothing gets installed.


IF WINDOWS SHOWS A BLUE WARNING
-------------------------------

You may see "Windows protected your PC".

   Click  More info  ->  Run anyway

This appears because the app is not code-signed, not because anything
was found on your computer. Code signing certificates cost several
hundred dollars a year, and this is a free tool.


IF IT DOESN'T START
-------------------

The first launch can take 10-20 seconds while Windows scans the files.
Give it a moment before double-clicking again.


WHAT IT DOES
------------

Paste a column of numbers - monthly revenue, daily Rx count, inventory
units, active patients - and it tests seven forecasting methods against
your own history. It scores each one on data it was never allowed to
see, then tells you in plain English which to trust.

You need at least 20 numbers. 30 or more works much better.

Your data never leaves your computer. There is no server and no
account. You can unplug the internet and it still works.


THE AI MODELS (OPTIONAL)
------------------------

Inside the app there is a button to download Google TimesFM and Amazon
Chronos. That is a one-time 1.3 GB download and it is entirely
optional - the seven built-in methods work without it, and on monthly
pharmacy data they are usually just as accurate.
